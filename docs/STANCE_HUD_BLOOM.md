# Stationary bloom and stance-following status

Translation card: tighten sustained standing-still fire, keep the split status display beside the player in crouch/prone, and make health/ammo/counts readable. Acceptance: stationary standing expansion is 80% of its former maximum; baseline, moving, airborne and crouch/prone caps are unchanged. HUD moves down across the three stances and remains inside the viewport. Owners: existing ReticleBloomEnvelope/WeaponBallistics for shot envelope and SocomLoadoutCardHUD for UI. Protected: camera, movement, animation, cadence, damage and network authority. Proof: stance_hud_bloom_regression, stance_bloom_cap_regression, contextual_hud_regression, bloom_shot_envelope_regression and rendered canonical main stance captures.

MERGE into existing shared profiles. The old cap had no stationary-standing distinction; the HUD used collision height (which may lag network stance) and one fixed vertical offset. The candidate reads canonical get_posture_name, uses saved stance heights/offsets and larger drawing metrics. No second transform or ballistics writer is introduced.

The standing cap blends back to the original cap by 15% normalized movement speed, avoiding a binary edge at the first movement frame. Both local and server ballistics pass their movement/airborne inputs into the same envelope. `factor *= lerpf(stationary, 1.0, moving)` in scripts/weapons/reticle_bloom_envelope.gd is the bounded change; spread geometry and aim center remain shared.

The HUD uses `camera.unproject_position(torso)` in scripts/ui/socom_loadout_card_hud.gd, following the [Godot Camera3D projection contract](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_camera3d.html#class-camera3d-method-unproject-position). Crouch/prone use lower world anchors and smaller upward screen offsets. UI smoothing does not write player or camera state. Saved profile metrics increase HP text 12→22, ammo 15→22, grenade counts 12→18, health radius 39→50 and thickness 3→5; display scale increases 0.70→0.85.
