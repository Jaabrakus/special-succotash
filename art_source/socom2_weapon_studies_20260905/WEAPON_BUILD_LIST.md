# SOCOM II — full weapon build checklist

43 handheld weapons and variants in the working roster. This is the complete
build queue, not a claim that all 43 meshes are finished. Ten pistol preview
models are built (including the accepted P9S); 33 other entries remain queued.
M11 and SP-10 are provisional. Nothing here replaces weapons in the game.

[Open the editable Blender pistol collection](pistol_batch/socom2_pistols_review.blend)
and [the rendered contact sheet](pistol_batch/pistol_contact_sheet.png).
Individual `.glb` exports are alongside the Blender collection.

Source video: [Feral Cat — SOCOM II PS2 All Weapons Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oI3NiRLeRhs).
Roster cross-checks: [SOCOM II weapon index](https://www.imfdb.org/wiki/SOCOM_II_U.S._Navy_SEALs)
and [552 Silenced / SOCOM II weapon navigation](https://socom.fandom.com/wiki/552_Silenced).
Names below follow the game's aliases; SD, launcher-equipped, and other visually
distinct variants have separate entries. Exact HUD frames are still being
confirmed for queued items. No guessed timestamps are presented as verified.

## Review standard

Model the bottom-left HUD's visible shape, not an unrelated modern replacement.
Each model needs a readable silhouette, separate material regions, side and
three-quarter inspection, and an editable Blender source. Hidden surfaces are
artistic estimates. These are exterior-only game props in arbitrary art units.
Any later game integration requires a separate approved pass for scale, grips,
sockets, animation and canonical runtime verification.
